**[Kleoth 0.5.5](https://github.com/ofcRS/kleoth/releases/tag/v0.5.5)** is out.


### Added

- **Kleoth updates itself.** Once a day it checks for a new version. When there is one, the
  menu-bar popover says "New version of Kleoth is available" with an **Update** button, and right
  after launch Kleoth shows the release notes itself. One more click installs the update and
  reopens Kleoth. It never installs in the middle of a meeting, its transcription, a screen
  recording or a dictation: it waits until they finish. Updates are signed, and Kleoth installs
  only an update signed with its own key. The check reads a small file from Kleoth's GitHub
  releases and sends nothing about you or your data. Check by hand, or turn the daily check off,
  in Settings → General → Updates (where the version is shown too).
- This version still has to be installed by hand. Updates after it arrive in the app.
